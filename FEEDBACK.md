# Feedback and issues

Tell us how Blank Box is working for you, what was confusing and what you’d like to see next.

[Open a report](https://github.com/blankboxcode/blankbox-community/issues/new/choose) or [search existing issues](https://github.com/blankboxcode/blankbox-community/issues).

## Choose a report

| Report | Use it for |
| --- | --- |
| Bug report | Something failed or behaved differently from what you expected. |
| Setup help | Installation, sign-in, source folders, networking or recovery instructions are unclear. |
| Feedback or feature idea | A workflow could be clearer, or you have an idea for a future change. |
| Share your experience | Describe using Blank Box on your devices, with your storage or as part of your everyday routine. |

For a bug or setup question, include the exact Blank Box version, operating system, installation method, browser/device, action, expected result and actual result. Include a short reproduction using sample names if possible. If you do not know a detail, say so rather than guessing.

Search for an existing report first. If you find the same problem, add your version and result there. Keep a different problem in a separate issue so we can track the fix.

## Keep your household private

Issues are public. Use names such as `Sample Album` or `Example Book`, and replace private paths with `/path/to/source`. Do not upload catalogs, backups, media, recovery keys, passwords, provider tokens or session files. Crop or redact screenshots and log excerpts before posting; logs can contain titles, paths and addresses. A small example is enough; keep your actual library private.

For recovery questions, report the step and outcome rather than the underlying household records.

## What to expect

We read reports as time allows. We may ask for a small example or more details about your setup. We can’t promise a response time or that every suggestion will be added, but your feedback helps us decide what to work on.

Find the installation packages on the [Downloads page](https://github.com/blankboxcode/blankbox-community/releases/latest) and everyday guidance in the [wiki](https://github.com/blankboxcode/blankbox-community/wiki). Windows remains experimental. Use the guides shipped with your installed version for its exact commands.
