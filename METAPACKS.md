A complete recovery point can be larger because it preserves the exact installed pack assets paired with that catalog, including versions retained from earlier pack updates. Keep all files in a recovery point together; removing older assets can make its catalog impossible to restore.

Movies retain 2,651 retired record IDs as aliases. Book edition coverage is partial; an ISBN candidate still requires review. Music covers an artist cohort, not every album, and contains no CD track-layout matching database. No artwork, media bytes, Game or Comic database is included. Supplied facts and named rating sources remain attributed. Missing facts remain unknown.

Movie and TV genre facts can suggest editable collections anchored in your household. Suggested groups describe known references and are not promises of complete franchises. Book/Music genres are not inferred. Confirm a match or collection to retain its facts in your household catalog, independently of the pack.

Each pack includes its version, compatible reader version, source information and publisher signature. Music core facts are CC0. Other included pack materials use the distribution permissions described in LICENSE, subject to third-party rights. Rights in third-party works and trademarks are not transferred.

Settings → Metadata supports removal and manual installation. Core also works with zero packs. Removing a pack retains confirmed facts, owner edits and collections. A later Core installation never silently reinstalls a pack you removed or replaces a version you chose. See UPDATES.md for authenticated updates and paired recovery.
