# Packaging, artwork and digital copies

## Packaging and release labels

Physical intake and **Your editions → Edit this copy** provide separate **Packaging** and **Release label** fields. Choose a suggested value or type your own.

Packaging describes the case or package: Steelbook, Mediabook, Digibook, Amaray, slipcover, slipbox, or box set. Release label identifies a collector release such as Manta Lab or WeET. Both stay with the physical edition. Movie studios, disc formats, cuts and copy condition retain their existing fields.

## More than one cover

Select an edition in item details and choose **Edit artwork**. The gallery starts with that edition; choose **This title** in **Attach to** for title artwork. Attach images to the title or a physical edition, select front, back, spine, disc or other, and upload one or several JPG, PNG or WebP images. You can keep up to 12 images per edition and 48 per title.

We save bounded private JPEG copies without photo metadata. Original image and media files stay unchanged. Select **Use as main cover** to choose the main Blank Box cover. If you currently use Plex or Jellyfin details, select Blank Box in **Choose details source** to display that local cover.

Front/back and other saved images have labelled thumbnails beside the main artwork. Select a thumbnail to view it, or choose **View full image** to open its full saved size. Removing a gallery image requires confirmation; removing the main image clears the local cover without deleting the other gallery images.

## Digital purchases and codes

In item details, use **Digital purchases & codes → Add record** or **Edit records**. Add a platform such as Movies Anywhere or Apple TV / iTunes, then choose **Purchased**, **Redeemed**, or **Code included**. Add an optional platform/title link and notes. You can associate the record with a physical copy when a disc package supplied the code.

These are your saved records. Blank Box does not access retailer accounts, verify entitlement, redeem codes, or play protected streams. Keep actual redemption codes separately. A code included in a package is distinct from a redeemed digital copy. The library card shows a **Digital record** badge; the platform and status appear in **Digital purchases & codes**, separately from playable files and connected apps.

## Edit service links

Open **Settings → Connections → Streaming and digital service links**. Select built-in services, including Movies Anywhere, edit a name or website link, or add a custom service. Use **Save settings** to apply your changes. You can restore a built-in service's default name and link.

Built-in title searches remain available with default links. An edited or custom website link opens the address you supplied. These shortcuts do not establish that a particular title is available or owned on that platform. Record your purchases and redemptions on the title itself.

Packaging, release labels, digital records and uploaded images are retained in complete catalog exports and library recovery. Linked source-drive originals remain separate and are not recreated by a catalog restore.
